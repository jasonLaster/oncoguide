Scientific guardrails:
- Each of the 50 cancer cells carries exactly one variant copy in both mixtures. Normal cells carry no variant copy.
- Upper mixture: 50×2 cancer copies plus 50×2 normal copies = 200 local copies.
- Lower mixture: 50×1 cancer copies plus 50×2 normal copies = 150 local copies.
- The numerator stays 50; the DNA-copy denominator changes.
- Cell purity is 50 cancer cells among 100 total cells. Cancer cell fraction (CCF) is 50 carrier cancer cells among 50 cancer cells, hence 100% in both panels.
- The VAF values are expected fractions under an idealized model with diploid normal cells, uniform local cancer-cell copies and unbiased recovery/sequencing observations; this is not a direct census of sequence reads.
- Do not portray 25% or 33% as the fraction of cancer cells, and do not portray CCF 100% as sample purity.
- The single cell icons are explicitly illustrative. Do not invent 50 literal icons, divide them into new clones, show cell proliferation, or suggest the mixtures are longitudinal observations.
- Copy bars represent copies of one local DNA region, not whole-genome ploidy or gene activity.
- No gene-function claim, treatment implication, patient-specific conclusion or therapeutic arrow.

Negative constraints: no photorealism, medical photography, people, organs, logos, watermarks, gradients, anatomical detail, decorative DNA helices or additional text. Do not add good/bad labels to the two panels. Use only the exact short labels specified above.
