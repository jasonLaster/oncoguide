The canonical bulk diagram explains changed pooled RNA from mixture. This page-specific extension adds two explicit immune-subset versus whole-sample denominators while keeping pooled RNA/model inference separate.
Four panels use stipulated 100-cell samples: A has 10 T cells among 20 immune cells, B has 30 among 60. Both immune-subset fractions are 50 percent, while whole-sample T-cell fractions are 10 and 30 percent. A separate pooled RNA and reference-model scene labels RNA contribution as different evidence from these supplied cell counts.
Caption: Name the denominator first: the same relative immune fraction can accompany very different whole-sample fractions. Counts here are supplied for teaching, not recovered by deconvolution.
