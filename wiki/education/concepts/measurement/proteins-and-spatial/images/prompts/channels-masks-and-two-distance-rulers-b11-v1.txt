The canonical segmentation art teaches merged, split and displaced masks. This extension keeps those distinctions and adds center-versus-boundary rulers on the same fixed geometry.
Separate synthetic blue and rose marker channels are aligned to one fictional tissue field. An overlay adds dashed estimated cell boundaries and two different rulers: center-to-center crosses the cells, while boundary-to-boundary spans only the gap. The bottom panel separates measured channels, estimated masks and calculated distances without claiming contact or recognition.
Caption: A distance needs a definition: channels are measured, masks are estimated, and proximity alone does not establish recognition.
