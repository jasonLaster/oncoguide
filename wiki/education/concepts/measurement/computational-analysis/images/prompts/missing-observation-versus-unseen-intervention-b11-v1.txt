The canonical validation art compares cell, donor and intervention partitions. This extension uses those distinct holdout units to contrast measurement completion with a never-seen intervention response and marks every model output as computed.
Two fictional task panels show training/reference pairs and reserved tests. In measurement completion, an image from a held-out donor has missing RNA filled with a computed diamond. In intervention prediction, measured responses to familiar X inform a prediction for Y whose response was withheld from development. A final panel says donor and intervention holdouts test different claims, and computed outputs must be compared with independent measurements.
Caption: Filling a missing observation and forecasting an unseen intervention are different tasks. The reference population and reserved test must match the claim.
Flat outlined scientific shapes, rounded panels, repeated toy objects, sans-serif 26px body labels. Theme geometry and data identical. No protocol, optimization, therapeutic design, sequence or dose.
