Do not show cytochrome c emerging from the matrix or crossing the inner membrane. Do not imply that inner-membrane morphology never changes. No expression-to-dependency or clinical-benefit inference; no drug efficacy ranking or prescribing. Apoptosis does not automatically prove immunogenicity.
