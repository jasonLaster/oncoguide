All labels specified below must be readable when the whole image is embedded at 326 CSS pixels wide.

The sequence is a schematic annotation, not literal amino-acid letters. Directly label the blue segment "Domain A" and sage segment "Domain B". Label the chain "One chain". Small branching dotted guide lines, without arrowheads, connect this sequence to the two hypothetical arrangements below, so these are alternatives, not sequential construction stages.

Keep each domain's internal folded shape identical across both scenes, with only Domain B's rotation and placement changed. A single uninterrupted ochre flexible linker physically connects domain A to domain B in EACH scene. Do not add a third domain or detach the chain.

A light dashed oval highlights the possible pocket in that gap. A short leader labels that cleft "Proposed pocket?". Make it visually clear that BOTH domain surfaces form this possible pocket. Both scenes are equally plausible hypothetical alternatives, no correct/incorrect colors, no transition arrow between them.

Bottom: two short lines, "Local folds: confident" and "Arrangement: uncertain". These refer to the fictional prediction, not verified experimental folds. Below them place the takeaway "Local confidence is not enough". Keep this readable, without boxes or a separate text-heavy legend.
