## Reading a cell's condition

To read whether a cell has stopped, been damaged or died, distinguish [apoptosis and its B-cell lymphoma 2 (BCL-2) family control](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/cell-death-and-survival/apoptosis-and-bcl2-family), [necrosis](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/cell-death-and-survival/necrosis) and [senescence](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/cell-death-and-survival/senescence). Each needs different evidence.
