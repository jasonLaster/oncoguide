# Learn the biology and evidence

**Choose the question you want to understand, then follow a path in order.** These pages teach concepts and how to read evidence. Current results and treatment choices are linked from each guide’s case box.

## Begin with your goal

| Your question | Learning path |
|---|---|
| What do surgery results mean? | [Read pathology and surgery results](/wiki/education/guides/reading-pathology-and-surgery-results/index): pCR, RCB, margins and staging |
| How does ordinary treatment work? | [Understand standard treatment](/wiki/education/guides/treating-early-tnbc/index): drug classes, surgery, radiation and immune side effects |
| What makes an immune treatment work? | [Understand immunotherapy](/wiki/education/guides/how-the-immune-system-fights-cancer/index): cells, display, recognition, access and escape |
| Can I trust this trial or test claim? | [Read evidence](/wiki/education/guides/reading-cancer-evidence/index): endpoints, curves, hazard ratios, PPV and clinical utility |
| What does multi-omics add? | [Understand multi-omics](/wiki/education/reading-a-tumor/omics-and-multi-omics): molecular layers, cells, space, function and time |
| How is a personalized vaccine designed? | [Design a personalized vaccine](/wiki/education/guides/personalized-cancer-vaccine/index): discovery, presentation, selection, manufacture and response evidence |
| How do cell therapies differ? | [Cellular therapies](/wiki/education/guides/engineered-cell-therapy/index): CAR, TCR, TIL, NK and in-vivo engineering |
| What does a tumor model actually predict? | [Modeling tumor response](/wiki/education/modeling-tumor-response/index): physical and computational models, controls and validation |
| How do treatment formats reach a target? | [Targeted modalities](/wiki/education/targeted-therapy-modalities/index): antibody, payload, engager, radioligand and engineered cell |
| Can local injury create an immune response? | [Local treatment](/wiki/education/local-treatment-modalities/index): delivery, immune signals and matched clinical evidence |
| What does injection into a tumor change? | [Intratumoral therapy](/wiki/education/intratumoral-therapy): route, agents and trial claims |
| How do repair and survival pathways connect? | [PARP and cell survival](/wiki/education/molecular-profiling/parp-and-cell-survival/index): repair, recycling, growth signals and experiments |

If all of this is new, begin with **standard treatment**, then **pathology** and **immunotherapy**. Use the multi-omics vocabulary course when a report introduces unfamiliar cell or molecular terms; it is a supporting reference within the multi-omics path.
