## Review and maintenance

The October 2026 corrections replaced misleading illustrations of a fixed adult TCR repertoire, an outdated six-group subtype model, an HRD treatment shortcut and chemotherapy mechanisms in their teaching pages. Historical files and prompts are retained separately from this active-use register. Unused files are not automatically safe to delete, and remaining artwork and missing prompts require individual review. See [the audit implementation record](/project-management/audits/2026-10-08-education-content-audit/implementation) for the replacement list and outstanding work.
