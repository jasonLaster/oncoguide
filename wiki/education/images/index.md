## Review and maintenance

The October 2026 corrections replaced misleading illustrations of a fixed adult TCR repertoire, an outdated six-group subtype model, an HRD treatment shortcut and chemotherapy mechanisms in their teaching pages. Historical files and prompts are retained separately from this active-use register. Unused files are not automatically safe to delete, and remaining artwork and missing prompts require individual review.
