# Pathway mechanisms: a supporting lesson library

**Learn how to test a pathway hypothesis by separating amount, activity, dependence, and clinical effect.**

These five lessons support [From a variant to a drug claim](/wiki/education/guides/from-variant-to-drug/index). Read them in order for a broad view, or choose the circuit relevant to your question. Each begins with its prerequisite concepts and ends with a worked experiment and practice answer.

The familiar legacy URLs are preserved so existing links continue to work. The lessons teach general biology through fictional scenarios. They do not contain report rankings, individual results, or a list of proposed treatment combinations.

## The organizing questions

First, ask what was actually measured: a sequence change, an RNA program, a protein state, or a functional response. Then ask what direction the finding implies and whether that direction was tested. Finally, distinguish an active process from a process the cancer cells need to survive.

## Concepts for each circuit

Recycling: [autophagy](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/cell-death-and-survival/autophagy) and [ubiquitin and the proteasome](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/cell-death-and-survival/ubiquitin-and-proteasome).

Growth and supply: [cholesterol homeostasis through SCAP–SREBP](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/metabolism-and-stress/cholesterol-and-srebp) and [PI3K–AKT–mTOR](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/signaling-pathways/pi3k-akt-mtor).

Cell messages: [Notch](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/signaling-pathways/notch-signaling) and [Wnt–β-catenin](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/signaling-pathways/wnt-beta-catenin).

Stress and energy: [NRF2–KEAP1](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/signaling-pathways/nrf2-keap1), [ferroptosis](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/cell-death-and-survival/ferroptosis), and [oxidative phosphorylation](/wiki/education/concepts/cancer-biology/metabolism-and-stress/oxidative-phosphorylation).
